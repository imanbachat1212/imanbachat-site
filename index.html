In Lebanon by Whish Money, OMT or bank transfer; abroad by bank transfer, Wise or PayPal. Projects start with a 50% deposit and the rest is due before launch.

How long does it take?+

A landing page usually takes one to two weeks, a full site two to four, and WhatsApp AI two to four depending on what it handles. You get the timeline before we start.

Do you work with clients outside Lebanon?+

Yes: Lebanon, the UAE, Australia and Canada so far. Everything is done remotely, with calls in your time zone.

No, it works on its own. The two work best together: the site brings people in and the AI handles them.
