Privacy policy

Last updated 21 November 2026

This site, imanbachat.com, belongs to Iman Bachat, a web developer in Tripoli, Lebanon. It's a simple portfolio, and it collects as little as possible.

What this site collects

Nothing that identifies you. There are no accounts, no cookies, no analytics and no advertising trackers. The price calculator runs in your browser and doesn't send your choices anywhere.

Third-party services

Hosting: the site is hosted on Netlify, which keeps standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security and reliability.
Fonts: text fonts load from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers.
Booking a call: the "Book a call" buttons open Cal.com. Anything you enter there is handled under Cal.com's privacy policy and shared with me only to arrange the call.

If you contact me

When you email me or book a call, I use your name, contact details and message only to reply and discuss your project. I don't sell or share them, and I delete them on request.

Your rights and questions

To ask what I hold about you, or to have it deleted, use the email link in the footer of the homepage.
